About Me

So, who am I?

Hi, I'm Wisnu, an Informatics student at the Faculty of Engineering, Universitas Tanjungpura, with a big interest in game development. I've been drawn to the world of technology since I was a kid, and my curiosity was never just about how to use things, but about how they actually work.

Besides games, I'm also interested in software engineering, and I've just started learning web development. For my game projects, I use the Unity game engine, with C# as my main programming language.

My goal is to become a successful game developer and make both of my parents proud.
